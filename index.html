Questions

The ones owners actually ask.

Do I need a tech team to work with you?

No. Our plans are built for owners without IT staff. We handle the setup, the tools and the training, and explain everything in plain English.

How much does it cost?

Monthly plans start at $299 (Starter) and $699 (Growth). A one-time Cloud & AI Audit is $2,500, and larger builds are scoped after a free 30-minute call. Monthly plans can be cancelled anytime.

Is my customers' data safe if we use AI?

Privacy comes first. We choose tools and settings so your business data stays under your control, and we've built HIPAA, SOC 2, GDPR and ISO 27001 aligned architectures for regulated industries.

How quickly will we see results?

A first AI automation typically goes live in about two weeks, and the cloud audit gives you a written roadmap in five business days.

Do I own what you build?

Yes. You own all code and documentation, and we include knowledge transfer so you're never locked in.

Do you only work with local businesses?

We're based in Lowell, MA and love working with businesses across Greater Boston and the Merrimack Valley, in person when it helps. We also serve clients across the United States remotely.
